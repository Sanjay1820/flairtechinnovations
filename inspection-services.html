Accurate Inspection Integrated With Your Supply Chain

Flairtech Innovations delivers thorough on-site and terminal-based quality inspection services to catch dimensional, cosmetic, and packaging variances early—eliminating costly line stoppages and downstream returns.

  • Precision Dimensional Metrology: Digital caliper, micrometer, and tolerance verification.
  • Incoming & Pre-Shipment Quality Audits: Lot sampling, AQL checks, and compliance documentation.
  • Sorting, Containment & Rework Support: Rapid isolation of non-conforming parts at hubs or plants.
  • Export Packaging & Crating Inspection: Verification before coast-to-coast or cross-border dispatch.
